## 1. Core Philosophy
HoMee is a high-end, autonomous ecosystem for on-demand services, designed with **Zero Error Tolerance**. It follows the **L-CODE Dynamics** principles: Military-grade security, High-ticket aesthetics, and AI-driven automation.

## 2. System Architecture (Clean Architecture)
The project is structured to ensure 100% scalability and zero bloat:
- **`domain/entities`**: Core business models (HoMee, Job).
- **`domain/use-cases`**: Business logic engines (SmartMatch, Billing, Industry validation).
- **`infrastructure/`**: Connectors (Supabase, Stripe, Hlidacky Mirror) and services (AI Support, Notifications).
- **`interfaces/`**: High-end UI components and screens (React Native Expo).
- **`shared/`**: Security kernels, premium themes, and identity wrappers.

## 3. Key Modules

### 🛡️ Identity & Trust (The Wall)
- **100% BankID Mandate**: Mandatory legal identification for all providers.
- **Certification Wall**: Segment-specific verification (Medical License for Physio, Criminal Record for Senior Care).
- **Zero Anonymity**: No profile is activated without verified identity traces.

### 🤖 Intelligence Engines
- **Smart Match Engine**: AI-driven weighted assignment (Rating vs Proximity). No manual picking.
- **Emergency Protocol**: Critical Priority for LOCKSMITH/GASTRO_RESCUE (arrival < 30 mins).
- **Support AI**: Automated issue resolution via GPT-4o-mini (GPS-aware conflict handling).

### 💰 Law & Finance (The Vault)
- **Stripe Escrow**: Payments held securely; released only after quality confirmation.
- **L-Code Dynamics Margin**: Fixed 25% platform fee automatically separated.
- **Smart Contract Factory**: Millisecond generation of signed contracts and insurance activation.
- **Legal Audit Log**: Full traceability for GDPR and legal compliance.
